# Scripture — decisions

Every verse in the app is **ESV, quoted whole**, pulled verse-by-verse from Bible Hub, with each reference linking to its page there. That part is settled.

The interactive version is the Claude artifact **The Verse Docket**
`https://claude.ai/artifact/GFV9R7Fwn8gsfAd6hs6V21` — decisions are stored in its database at `review/decisions`. That docket goes deeper than this file: it has per-verse grammar/context flags across the whole app, four things Michelle asked to add on her own (Philippians 4:6 promoted into the spine, a question about Galatians 6:8 vs. 6:9, the fuller Ephesians 6 armor passage, and an Elijah-at-Horeb panel from 1 Kings 19), and one open framing question — whether to name a hard thought as spiritual attack — that isn't a verse call at all. **That larger list hasn't been worked through with Michelle yet; only the items below have.**

---

## Ruled on

**2 Corinthians 10:5** — the title verse and the margin of page three. **KEEP.** Michelle's note: *"Yes — good verse for holding every thought captive."* She added that it should sit alongside a wider frame: Philippians 4:6, Galatians 6:9, Ephesians 6:16, and 1 Kings 19 (all now in the app).

**Jeremiah 29:11**, paired with *"I already know how this ends"*. **SWAPPED** to Psalm 31:15 — *"My times are in your hand; rescue me from the hand of my enemies and from my persecutors!"* First person, said from inside distress rather than promised over it.

**Galatians 1:10**, paired with *"I know what they're thinking about me"*. **SWAPPED** to Proverbs 29:25 — *"The fear of man lays a snare, but whoever trusts in the LORD is safe."* General address, squarely about the thing.

**Romans 8:1**, used twice (paired with *"I should be handling this better"* and in the "after you've failed" verse-library group). **CUT from both**, unpaired for now. Michelle's ruling: *"This doesn't fit here — that verse is about salvation,"* not performance or failure recovery.

**1 Samuel 16:7**, in "when you're not enough" — the one place the app trimmed a verse instead of quoting it whole (the full verse opens with God rejecting Eliab). **CUT entirely**, not trimmed and not moved.

**Micah 7:8**, in "after you've failed" — Zion personified taunting a national enemy; "O my enemy" doesn't map when the enemy is your own head. **CUT, no replacement.**

**Joshua 1:9**, in "when you need courage" — a commissioning for the conquest of Canaan, and the second "be strong and courageous" alongside Deuteronomy 31:6. **KEPT as-is.**

**Isaiah 1:18**, cut from "when you're ashamed" in an earlier rebuild for space. **RESTORED**, despite opening a covenant lawsuit whose conditions (vv. 19–20) aren't shown.

**2 Corinthians 10:12**, cut from "when you're comparing". **LEFT OUT** — about rival teachers in Corinth; the subject is "they," and "without understanding" lands harsh aimed at a struggling student.

**One-line context under every verse.** Every verse across the shapes, the four-step flow, the grounding page, and the verse library now carries a short line naming who it was originally spoken to and in what situation — including a plain note on the verses addressed corporately to Israel (Isaiah 41:10, Isaiah 43:2, Deuteronomy 31:6, Isaiah 40:31) rather than to an individual. Written from general biblical knowledge, not re-verified against Bible Hub the way the verse text itself was — worth a skim for anything that reads off.

## If you add a verse

Pull the exact ESV text from `https://biblehub.com/esv/<book>/<chapter>-<verse>.htm` and quote it whole. Do not write it from memory. Book slugs use underscores: `1_samuel`, `2_corinthians`, `1_kings`.
